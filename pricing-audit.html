Holiday-let pricing and calendar audit

One listing, checked line by line: nightly rates, minimum stays, gap nights, last-minute and far-out pricing, and the calendar settings that quietly cost bookings. You get a written report with numbered fixes, in order of money back. No calls, no sales pitch. Everything is in writing.

What I check

  • Base and peak rates against similar listings near you for the next 90 days
  • Minimum stays and whether they block bookable gaps
  • Orphan nights between bookings and how to fill them
  • Last-minute and far-out pricing: discounts, premiums, booking window
  • Weekly and monthly discounts, fees and how they show to guests
  • Calendar and availability settings that hide you from searches

What you get
