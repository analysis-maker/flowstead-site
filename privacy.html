Privacy policy

How Flowstead uses your information

Last updated 21 November 2026. This page explains what personal information Flowstead collects through this website and when you contact me, why, how long I keep it, and your rights under UK data protection law (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

Who I am

Flowstead is run by Ian McDermott as a sole trader in the United Kingdom. I am the controller of the personal information described here. You can contact me by email at hello@flowstead.co.uk.

What I collect

  • When you email me: your name, email address, and anything you choose to include in your message.
  • When you book a call through Calendly: your name, email address, the time you pick and any answers you give on the booking form. Calendly processes this on my behalf and sends me the booking details.
  • During an audit or project: business contact details and information about how your business works that you choose to share.
  • The free checklist: it is a PDF you download from this site. The download does not ask for your name, email or any other details. If you email me to ask for a copy, I use your email address only to send it and to answer any follow-up.
  • Website analytics: only if you click Accept on the cookie banner, Google Analytics records how the site is used (pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type). If you decline, analytics cookies are not set. Your choice is stored in your browser's local storage so the banner doesn't keep appearing.
  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as IP addresses for security and to keep the service running.

Why I use it, and the lawful basis

  • To reply to your enquiry, run the free audit and send the written map: legitimate interests (responding to people who contact me) or steps before entering a contract at your request.
  • To deliver and invoice paid work: performance of a contract, and legal obligation for tax and accounting records.
  • For website analytics: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser and choosing Decline.
  • I do not sell your information or use it for automated decisions that have legal effects on you. Details collected through this website are not used to sign you up for marketing emails.

Who I share it with

Only with the services I use to run Flowstead, acting on my instructions: the service that hosts my email, Calendly (bookings), Google (analytics, only after you consent) and GitHub (website hosting). Some of these services are based outside the United Kingdom. I may also need to share information if the law requires it.

How long I keep it

  • Enquiries and audit notes that don't lead to work: up to 2 years after our last contact, then deleted.
  • Client project and invoicing records: 6 years after the end of the tax year they relate to, as required for UK tax records.
  • Analytics data: Google Analytics runs only after you accept cookies. How long that data is kept is the retention period configured in Google Analytics, which can be changed.

Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of your information, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to how I use it, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Email hello@flowstead.co.uk and I will reply within one month. There is no charge.

If you are unhappy with how I have handled your information, please tell me first so I can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Client data in automations I build

When I build automations inside a client's own accounts, the client is the controller of their guests' and customers' data and I act as their processor. We'll agree a data processing agreement with clients where needed. I use the minimum access needed, and nothing is shared between clients.

Changes to this policy

If I change how I use personal information, I will update this page and the date at the top.
