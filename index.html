FAQ

Good questions.

Will an AI receptionist annoy my guests?

It's built to be short and polite, to read details back, and never to guess at prices or availability. Anything it can't handle goes to you with a summary, so the guest doesn't repeat themselves.

Do I need to change my software?

Usually not. I build on top of what you already use: your PMS, helpdesk, Gmail, Google Sheets, Slack.

Who owns the automations?

You do. Everything is built in your own accounts, documented, and handed over with a walkthrough video.

What about guest data and GDPR?

Data stays in your systems. I use the minimum access needed, and nothing is shared between clients.

Do you only work with holiday lets?

That's my specialism, but the same systems suit letting agents, property managers, cleaners and other small service businesses.
