Holiday-let time leak calculator

A rough picture of the guest messages and calls your team handles by hand, and what comes back if a cautious share of the routine work is handled automatically. The sums run in your browser. Nothing is stored or sent.

Working days use a 7.5-hour day. Hours are shown to one decimal place. Pounds are rounded to the nearest pound.

How the sums work

  • Message hours per month = bookings × threads × minutes ÷ 60
  • Call hours per month = calls per week × 4.33 × minutes ÷ 60
  • Hours back = (message hours + call hours) × share handled automatically
  • £ per month = hours back × hourly cost
  • £ per year = £ per month × 12
  • Working days = hours back ÷ 7.5

Why 40%

In one live inbox for an Australian holiday-rental manager, about 47% of the inbox now closes with no human, and 10,000+ guest emails have been handled automatically. The default here is set lower, at 40%, to stay cautious.

What's left out

Set-up and running costs, and time spent checking drafts, aren't included. Complaints, damage and refunds still go to a person. Nothing is stored or sent.
